Oilers Line Combinations

Below we’ve broken down each of the Edmonton Oilers line combinations by position group. Here you can see all four forward lines, three defence lines, as well as the current goalie depth chart.

Forward

Sporting one of the best forward groups in the NHL, there’s no shortage of star power to be found in the forward lines for the Oilers. Headlined by Hart Trophy winners Connor McDavid and Leon Draisaitl, the Oilers have fantastic depth and skill in all four lines.

The Oilers current top line of Ryan Nugent-Hopkins, Connor McDavid, and Zach Hyman has been the most effective during the 2024-25 season, having scored eight goals while on the ice together.

Defence

They may not be as strong in comparison to the forward groups, but the Oilers still sport solid depth along the blue line with their defence pairings. With Matias Ekholm and Evan Bouchard leading the top defensive pairing on the squad, this combination of puck-moving ability and defensive prowess is hard to come by.

Having played every game together this season, Ekholm and Bouchard are a well-oiled machine, having contributed to the third-most goals scored of any defensive pairing across the NHL this season.

Goalies

A weak spot for the Oilers in recent years, the goaltender depth for this year’s team has been an issue. The Oilers sit with a bottom 10 ranking in save-percentage across the NHL, even though they possess one of the lowest goals-against averages.

With starts between the pipes going to Stuart Skinner and Calvin Pickard, this is an area that the Oilers are likely going to look to upgrade in the future.

Power Play

Essential in any team’s ability to win games is possession of a strong power play unit. With a man-advantage on the ice, this is a golden opportunity for squads to light the lamp and get an edge on the scoreboard.

With goal-scoring being the name of the game, the Oilers have been uber-aggressive when it comes to their power play unit utilizing four forwards and one defenceman on their top special teams line. Even with plenty of talent scattered across their power play lines, the Oilers currently rank in the bottom-half of the NHL in both power play goals scored, and power play-percentage.

Penalty Kill

If your team is going to be successful, having a reliable penalty kill unit is essential. Being down a man on the ice and getting away unscathed on the scoreboard can provide major relief for any team in a tightly-contested tilt.

Penalty kill lines are generally reserved for a team’s fastest skaters, as well as those with the best defensive ability as they’ll need to cover ample space as well as deter their opponent from generating dangerous scoring chances.

The Oilers penalty kill unit currently ranks in the bottom-five of the NHL in penalty kill percentage, only keeping opposing teams off the scoresheet 73.1% of the time. This is a troubling trend given that the Oilers have served fourth-least amount of penalty minutes across the NHL in 2024.

Injuries

One of the biggest factors in altering the lines of any hockey team, injuries can play a major role in shaping a team’s depth chart. If a top player goes down due to injury, this can cause a massive ripple effect all the way down the roster, such as a second line player being promoted to the top line, with replacements then shifting from the bottom up.

Oilers Lines FAQs

How often do the Oilers change their lines?

The Oilers change their lines from game-to-game. Whether it’s due to a strategic reason or roster changes such as injuries or trades, the Oilers coaching staff try to ensure that they have a competitive advantage every game so changes to their lines happen often.

Who plays on the Oilers’ top power play unit?

The Oilers top power play unit consists of Zach Hyman, Ryan Nugent-Hopkins, and Leon Draisaitl as forwards, with Evan Bouchard and Connor McDavid as defenceman. The Oilers possess sensational offensive depth in their forward groups so they opt to use four forwards in their top line rather than the traditional three forwards, two defenceman split.

How do injuries affect the Oilers’ line combinations?

Injuries have a major effect on the Oilers line combinations. If a player is ruled out of an NHL game due to injury, this can lead to the entirety of the Oilers lines tonight changing as they need to adjust for the overall shift in their roster.

Who is number 2 on Oilers?

The number 2 is donned by defenceman Evan Bouchard on the Edmonton Oilers. Bouchard is one of the top NHL players on the Oilers roster and has spent all six years of his NHL career with the Oilers.

Who is the captain of the Oilers hockey team?

The current captain of the Edmonton Oilers is Connor McDavid. McDavid made history in 2016 when he became the youngest player to be named captain in NHL history at just 19 years old.

Who are Connor McDavid’s linemates?
